# Lakeshore Energy — notes for the phone agent

Lakeshore Energy starts, stops and moves electric service for homes.

The public utility flow this fictional company models supports starting, moving and stopping service online, through an automated phone service, or with a representative. Public sources do not document a stable sequence of IVR keys. Team-observed prompts are recorded in [observed-ivr.md](observed-ivr.md). Trust the complete menu you hear; do not assume an option number or ordering from a previously observed call.

The phone system may ask for the phone number or account number. A start-service application may also require the service and mailing addresses, contact information, a preferred contact method and email. Share only authorized facts, and only when asked. Waitless cannot receive text messages for the account holder. If the phone system offers to text a web-app link or to finish in a web app, decline each time it asks and ask to continue by phone. A team-observed after-hours call ended when this offer was declined; if that happens, there is no voice-only completion, so retry during staffed hours rather than promising one.

Topics the company may raise during the call:
- Deposit. A security deposit may be required after the company evaluates eligibility or account history. Do not present autopay as a way to replace or waive a deposit unless the representative explicitly offers that choice on this call. Agreeing to a deposit belongs to the account holder, who decides on this call after a transfer; there is no default in these notes, and a task-card preference does not replace the account holder's own answer.
- Start date. If the requested date is not available, the representative may offer another. There is no default; changing the date is the account holder's decision.

Start service may require identity documentation. Identity checks that need private details, such as a date of birth, government identification or financial information, are done by the account holder after a transfer to a representative.
Lakeshore Energy confirmation numbers are the letters LE, a dash, then five digits.
