# Bluefin Harbor Bank — notes for the phone agent

Bluefin Harbor Bank handles questions about charges on its credit cards.

How the phone menu has usually sounded (it can change; trust what you hear):
- The main menu offers card charges and statements as one option, usually after account balances. Lost or stolen cards are a separate option.
- The charges option leads to a second menu where asking about a charge on a statement is usually read first.

What the virtual card assistant usually asks, one question at a time, out loud:
- The full name on the account and the phone number on the account. Say them aloud from your authorized facts; nothing is typed on the keypad here.
- Which charge the call is about: the merchant, the date and the amount. Answer from the goal in the task data; if a detail is not there, say you do not have it.

After that the assistant transfers the case to a card specialist. Waiting for a specialist can take several minutes of hold music; stay silent while it plays.

Topics the bank may raise:
- A callback. When specialists are busy, the specialist may offer to call the account holder back instead of waiting longer. Whether to accept a callback is the account holder's decision; there is no default. The specialist waits quietly while you check with the account holder.

The bank never needs a full card number on this call. Bluefin Harbor Bank reference numbers are the letters BH, a dash, then five digits. A reference number for a callback or a review is not a finished task: the charge still needs follow-up.
